Privacy policy

LetsJam, by Superapp B.V., Amsterdam, the Netherlands · last updated 18 November 2026

LetsJam is a free-to-play game where friends start pools on anything and call them with free coins. Coins have no cash value. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

Account: a random user ID created when you first open the app. We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.
Profile: the handle, display name, emoji, colour, city and bio you choose. Other players can see these.
Game activity: crews you're in, pools you create or join, your picks, votes, coin balance and history, friend requests and invite codes.
Reports and blocks: who you report or block and the reason you pick.
Technical: basic logs from our hosting providers (IP address, device type, timestamps) to keep the service running and secure.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos (unless you upload a clip), payment details or health data. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell data.

Why

To run the game (contract), to keep it safe and fair, including handling reports and preventing abuse (legitimate interest), and to meet legal obligations.

Who processes it

Supabase (database and login, EU, Frankfurt) and Vercel (web hosting). They process data only on our instructions.

How long

Until you delete your account. Reports are kept up to 12 months after they're handled. Server logs are kept up to 30 days.

Your rights

Delete your account any time in the app: Me → delete my account. This removes your profile, coins, friends and pool history straight away. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or to stop processing, at privacy@letsjam.app. You can complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Age

LetsJam is for adults 18+.
