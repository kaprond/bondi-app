LetsJam support

Friend pools with free coins. By Superapp B.V., Amsterdam.

How do pools work?
Make a crew, start a pool with a question and a stake in free coins, everyone picks an outcome. The creator proposes the result, the crew has 48 hours to confirm, and the winners split the pot. No majority means everyone gets their coins back.
Can I buy or cash out coins?
No. Coins are free, you get them every day, and they have no cash value.
Someone's being a pain
Open their pool and tap report this pool, or tap their profile and choose report or block. We check every report within 24 hours.
Delete my account
In the app: Me → delete my account. It's instant and permanent.
Where did bondi go?
The bondi e-bike app is discontinued. This app replaced it.
